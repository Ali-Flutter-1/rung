Confidence rarely arrives before you act. It often shows up after you do the small thing you have been putting off — and notice it went better than you expected.

Rung is a calm, private practice app for introverts and anyone who wants steadier social confidence: shyness, awkward moments, and the everyday fear of being seen. No lectures, no streak guilt, no feed. One small step at a time, at your pace.

HOW IT WORKS

Every step follows the same gentle loop:

1. PREDICT — before you do it, rate how intense you expect the moment to feel.
2. DO — go and do the small thing. That is it.
3. REFLECT — rate how it actually felt.

Then Rung shows you the gap between the two. For almost everyone, the worry is louder than the reality. Watching that gap shrink, in your own notes, changes what you believe about yourself faster than generic advice.

LADDERS THAT START WHERE YOU ARE

Six ladders cover situations that matter most: speaking up, everyday conversation, being seen, assertiveness, social events, and connection. Each begins somewhere genuinely small — sending a voice note, asking a shopkeeper a question, saying one thing in a meeting — and climbs only as fast as you do.

If a step feels too big, take an easier one. If your situation is not covered, build your own ladder with your own steps.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Six step-by-step ladders for real-world social situations
• The prediction gap — see how often fear overshoots reality
• A streak that forgives a missed day, because shame is not motivation
• Private support pods: small groups of people doing the same work
• Breathing and grounding tools for the moment before you walk in
• Small games for when your mind needs somewhere else to be
• Progress at a glance: steps cleared, best streak, where you are growing
• A gentle daily reminder, only if you want one

BUILT PRIVATE, ON PURPOSE

Your reflections are the most personal thing in this app, so they never leave your device. What you write stays in local storage on your phone — not on our servers, not in analytics, not anywhere we can read it.

There are no ads and no trackers selling your data to advertisers. Analytics are off unless you turn them on. You can delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the app, in two taps.

FREE, AND WHAT COSTS MONEY

The daily practice is free, and stays free. Every ladder's first ten steps, the prediction gap, streaks, reminders, breathing tools, and games — all free, with no trial timer.

Premium adds the parts that cost us money to run: more support pods, unlimited ladders of your own, and deeper steps for when you are ready to go further. If you never subscribe, the app still works.

WHO THIS IS FOR

If you rehearse phone calls before making them. If you leave the group chat on read because you cannot find the right words. If you have turned down something you wanted because of who would be there. If "just put yourself out there" has never once helped.

You do not need to become an extrovert. Social skills are practised, not inherited — you need smaller steps and evidence that you can take them.

AN HONEST NOTE

Rung is a lifestyle self-help practice tool for gradual, voluntary challenges — not therapy, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis or need clinical support, please contact qualified help in your area. Rung is meant to complement your own choices, never replace care you need.

START WITH ONE STEP

Not a transformation. Not a 30-day challenge. One small step, today, and an honest note about how it went.
