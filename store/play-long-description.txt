Social anxiety doesn't go away by waiting for confidence to arrive. It fades when you do the small thing you've been avoiding — and discover it went better than you feared.

Rung is a calm, private practice app for social anxiety, shyness and the everyday fear of being seen — built for introverts and anyone who finds people harder work than they let on. No lectures, no streak guilt, no feed. Just one small step at a time, at your pace.

HOW IT WORKS

Every step follows the same gentle loop:

1. PREDICT — before you do it, rate how anxious you expect to feel.
2. DO — go and do the small thing. That's it.
3. REFLECT — rate how it actually felt.

Then Rung shows you the gap between the two. For almost everyone, the fear is louder than the reality. Watching that gap shrink, in your own handwriting, changes what you believe about yourself far faster than any advice can.

LADDERS THAT START WHERE YOU ARE

Six ladders cover the situations that matter most: speaking up, everyday conversation, being seen, assertiveness, social events and connection. Each begins somewhere genuinely small — sending a voice note, asking a shopkeeper a question, saying one thing in a meeting — and climbs only as fast as you do.

Nothing is locked behind a wall of theory. If a step feels too big, take an easier one. If your situation isn't covered, build your own ladder with your own steps.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Six step-by-step ladders across the areas social anxiety touches most
• The prediction gap — see how much your fear overestimates reality
• A streak that forgives a missed day, because shame isn't motivation
• Private support pods: small, moderated groups of people doing the same work
• Breathing and grounding tools for the moment before you walk in
• Small games for when your mind needs somewhere else to be
• Your progress at a glance: steps cleared, best streak, where you're growing
• A gentle daily reminder, only if you want one

BUILT PRIVATE, ON PURPOSE

Your reflections are the most personal thing in this app, so they never leave your device. What you write stays in local storage on your phone — not on our servers, not in analytics, not anywhere we can read it.

There are no ads and no trackers selling your anxiety to advertisers. Analytics are off unless you turn them on. You can delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the app, in two taps.

FREE, AND WHAT COSTS MONEY

The daily practice is free, and stays free. Every ladder's first ten steps, the prediction gap, streaks, reminders, breathing tools and games — all free, forever, with no trial timer.

Premium adds the parts that cost us money to run: more support pods, unlimited ladders of your own, and deeper steps for when you're ready to go further. If you never subscribe, the app still works.

WHO THIS IS FOR

If you rehearse phone calls before making them. If you leave the group chat on read because you can't find the right words. If you've turned down something you wanted because of who'd be there. If "just put yourself out there" has never once helped.

You are not broken, and you don't need to become an extrovert. Social skills are practised, not inherited — you need smaller steps and evidence that you can take them.

AN HONEST NOTE

Rung is a self-help practice tool built on the principle behind graded exposure — the idea that facing something gradually, repeatedly, teaches your nervous system it is survivable. It is not therapy, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional care. If your anxiety is severe, please talk to a doctor or therapist. Rung works well alongside that, never instead of it.

START WITH ONE STEP

Not a transformation. Not a 30-day challenge. One small step, today, and an honest note about how it went.
