Delete your account and data

Last updated: September 2026

You can delete your Rung account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app. It takes two taps and does not require emailing anyone.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Rung and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted immediately.

If you can't sign in

Email alijutt12208025@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We will confirm within 30 days, usually much sooner. We ask you to write from that address so that nobody can delete someone else's account.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account and email addressDeleted immediately
Display name, bio, profile settingsDeleted immediately
Messages you posted in support podsDeleted immediately
Pod memberships, blocks and reports you madeDeleted immediately
Backed-up progress (streak, steps completed, anxiety ratings, check-ins, custom steps)Deleted immediately
Push notification tokens for your devicesDeleted immediately
Reflection and prediction notesNever stored on our servers — removed with the app when you uninstall it

Deletion is permanent. There is no recovery window and no backup we can restore from, so please be sure before you confirm.

What may briefly remain

Records we are required to keep, such as a purchase or refund record held by Apple or Google, remain with those payment providers under their own policies — we cannot delete those on your behalf. Routine server logs containing technical information are automatically removed within 30 days.

Deleting the app only

Uninstalling Rung removes everything held on your device, including all your notes. It does not delete your account on our servers — use the steps above for that.
